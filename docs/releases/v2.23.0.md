# v2.23.0 (2026-11-28)

Danh sách tính từ tag `v2.22.0` (2026-11-27). Gói US Market + SoDEX Wealth (#588, DEC-402 đến DEC-405).

## Cho người dùng (English, dùng được cho GitHub Release)

- US Market (new, from the banner in Strategy Lab): the new Anthropic markets on SoDEX (vANT spot and ANT-USD perps) with price, order book status and depth, and a button to start one of your DeskLabs bots on them. US fund tokens are listed as "Watching" while their order books are empty.
- SoDEX Wealth in DeskLabs, read-only: the US Market page lists SoDEX Wealth funds with NAV and fund size, and Portfolio › My Wealth shows what you hold on SoDEX Wealth (totals, funds, activity), read from SoDEX for your wallet with no signature. DeskLabs never invests, redeems or moves funds; "Invest on SoDEX" opens SoDEX Wealth.
- Thin order books: on the Anthropic and fund token markets, a new bot's total position can't be larger than the thinner side of the order book (top 10 price levels), and an order that fills at once to open or add is cut to 25% of the side it takes (Decisions shows "Order reduced") or waits when that side is empty. Closing orders, stop loss, take profit and Stop are never cut or held. Other markets are unchanged.
- SoDEX rewards card on Overview: "This week" shows your Spot and Perps volume for the running week as SoDEX reports them, and when you have no Blind Box to claim, how much more trading unlocks your next one.
- Signal Research (team): company news about Anthropic now maps to ANT-USD. Product, model and tooling news is read but not traded. A new "News by market" block shows each article's market, how it was read, what the rules decided and the order book at the time.
- SoDEX Wealth alerts (off for now, DeskLabs team testing first): a bell and Telegram message when an investment, redemption or recurring buy completes, fails, is rejected or cancelled, or when a recurring buy is skipped.

Not financial advice. Past results are not a forecast.

Build arg tuỳ chọn mới `SODEX_LOTTERY_GATEWAY` (trống = `https://mainnet-lottery.sodex.dev`, prod không cần đặt). Không migration.
