How a paper fill is priced
  • Entry at the SoDEX price after the lane’s real delay (now 34 s median), as a taker order.
  • Exits at take profit, stop loss or max hold, also as taker.
  • Costs on both legs: the market’s SoDEX taker fee, the builder fee, and the spread measured on SoDEX at that hour.
  • Paper results never mix with real-money numbers.
