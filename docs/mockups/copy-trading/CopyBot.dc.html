What happens after you start

  1. We wait for the trader's next trade. Positions they already hold are not copied.
  2. When they open, we open the same amount every time (your "each copied position"), however big their trade is. When they add, we add in the same proportion.
  3. When they cut or close, we cut or close yours too.
  4. If a position hits your stop loss, we close it and wait for their next trade.
Your results will differ from theirs You trade on SoDEX a few seconds later, at SoDEX prices, with your own size and leverage. Some trades get skipped when the price moves too far, and copies can be smaller or skipped when SoDEX liquidity is thin.
