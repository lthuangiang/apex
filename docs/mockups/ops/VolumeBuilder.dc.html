The Volume Builder tab of the Ops page. Volume Builder has no quotes and no request line, so its health is told by how rounds open and end. Under the table: how each bot entered, how its rounds ended, and why it waited.

How entries were sent (24 h)

Post-only = rests on the book, maker fee. Market = after N post-only misses in a row the bot sends a market order (“Market orders after”); with N = 0 every entry and exit is a market order.
