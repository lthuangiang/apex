What Claude can do with DeskLabs

It reads the same numbers you see in the app. It never acts on its own.

Read your account

Bots, portfolio, profit and loss, fees, and how much of your loss limit you've used. Practice bots are kept apart from real money.

Explain what happened

Why a bot paused, what it decided and when, what needs you on AI Desk, and how past changes worked out.

Look at traders

Browse Copy Trading lists and trader profiles, with the same filters and warnings as the app.

Draft bot settings

Claude can prepare a new bot or a change and give you a link. You review it in DeskLabs and decide.

What Claude can't do

Place or cancel orders, start, pause or change a bot, apply a draft, or withdraw and move funds. It never sees your keys or signatures, and it doesn't predict prices.

How drafts work

Claude never applies anything. You stay in control at every step.

  1. Step 1 Claude prepares a draft It checks the settings against the same rules as the app and gives you a link.
  2. Step 2 You open it in DeskLabs The form opens filled in, marked "From Claude". Drafts last 24 hours.
  3. Step 3 You review and decide Change anything, then save or start it yourself, with the usual checks. Or just close it.
