From an article to a closed paper trade, then into the stats that decide whether the signal is ever allowed near real money. One real-looking example runs through every step.

8The gate · when can we trust it?Rule
  • At least 100 closed trades per level is the floor. Once there is an average, the page shows how many trades that average really needs: a small edge needs more (≈ (1.96 × spread ÷ difference vs random)²).
  • Beats random entries at the same moments, by at least 2× the cost of a trade, with the likely range above 0.
  • Same sign in both halves of the period, so one lucky week can’t carry it.
  • At ~30 closed news trades a week, 3 levels × 100 ≈ 10 weeks to a first answer.
9Later · real moneyNeeds its own approval
Only a level that passes the gate gets a design for users. Real money is a separate decision, never automatic.
