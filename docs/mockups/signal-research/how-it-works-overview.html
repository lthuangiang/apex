Every 5 minutes DeskLabs reads new crypto news, keeps articles about a coin listed on SoDEX, asks Claude what kind of news it is, then each running version decides whether to open a fake-money trade. A watcher closes trades at TP, SL or max hold.

01Collectnew articles from SoSoValue

Every 5 minutes DeskLabs asks SoSoValue for new crypto news. Each article keeps two times: when the source published it and when DeskLabs got it. The gap is the source delay.

03ScoreClaude names the news type, the rules decide
When Claude is called

Right after Normalize, in the same 5-minute run. Oldest article first. Each article passes 4 checks before Claude sees it:

  1. Published over 24 h ago → dropped, stale news
  2. Over 30 min since it was published → dropped without asking Claude: a trade now would be too late
  3. This run has used its Claude calls → waits for the next run, in order
  4. Today’s Claude budget is used up → dropped

Claude then gets one article per call: the title, the first 4,000 characters, the source, the publish time and the candidate coins from step 2. It answers with one of those coins or none. A failed call is tried once more.

Claude picks one news type from a fixed list, the coin it directly hits, whether it is an ad, and the event date. Claude never decides to buy or sell.

DeskLabs then checks that the coin is written in the article, that it is not a treasury buy of BTC or ETH, and that the event is not over 24 h old.

The rules table sets the side and the max hold for that news type. The level comes from the sources carrying the story and how strong they are: High needs two or more independent sources including a top one; one top source or two mid sources is Medium; otherwise Low. It is fixed at the first article.

Claude reader v3 reads each article at step 3. Trades always open, but they only count once that reader has been checked: 50 random labels, at least 80% marked right. A new reader starts its count again. See Labels.

04Contextsave the moment, block nothing

Saves the answers to the context questions (research wallets, funding, price already ran) and the market state (BTC trend over 24 h, volatility over 4 h). Missing candles leave a blank, never a guess. See Context questions and Market state.

05Filtereach version’s own rules

One trade per story (coin + news type) per 7 days, the version’s minimum level and max source delay, fresh SoDEX prices, one open trade per coin, TP at least 2× the cost of a trade, then the version’s limits.

A trade blocked only by a limit is saved as a shadow trade, so we can see what the limit cost.

06Decideopen a fake-money trade

Opens at mid + half the spread + the taker fee, like a real fill. TP and SL come from the version’s multiples of the coin’s typical move over the max hold. See Take profit / Stop loss.

→Watcher and resultsclose, then measure

The watcher keeps reading SoDEX bid and ask and closes at TP, SL or max hold. Opposite news on the same coin: the version keeps or closes the trade, never flips it.

Closed trades show on this page and are compared with random entries at the same moments. See When a signal passes.

Never: no order is sent to SoDEX and no user money is touched. Claude only names the news type. The side, TP and SL come from the rules table and price data, so every decision can be replayed.
