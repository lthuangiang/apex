When opposite news arrives on an open trade
Opposite news = a new story on the same asset, the other direction, at or above "Lowest level to trade". Every version also records what closing at that moment would have made, so Keep and Close can be compared.

A signal stopped by a limit, or by too little fake balance, is not traded. It is still followed as a "would have" trade (reason "Limit: 20 open" or "Limit: N per day"), outside the balance, so no data is lost.
