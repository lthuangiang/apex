What counts as a news signal

Rules v2 · a starting guess that the results below prove or drop

A signal is one story about one SoDEX asset with one direction. More articles about the same story don’t open more trades; they raise its confidence. Confidence comes from how many independent sources report it and how trusted they are.

ConfidenceWhenSignalsPaper tradesAverage after feesVerdict so far
High2 or more independent sources within 30 min, at least one top-tier, all the same direction, nothing contradicting2114Not enough tradesNeeds 86 more trades
MediumOne top-tier source, or 2 or more mid-tier sources3817Not enough tradesNeeds 83 more trades
LowA single mid- or low-tier source3710Not enough tradesNeeds 90 more trades
How many trades until we trust a level? 100 closed trades per level. Below that the result shows “Not enough trades”, because a few lucky trades can look like an edge.
If a level fails, it stops being traded in the next rules version, and its trades stay in history under the old version.
