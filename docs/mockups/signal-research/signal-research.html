What counts as a news signal

Rules v2 · a starting guess that the results prove or drop

A signal is one story about one SoDEX asset, of a type on the list below. The type sets the direction; Claude only names the type and the asset. The bot decides on the first article and locks the confidence level at that moment. Articles that arrive later don’t open more trades; if they would have raised the level, the trade is tagged “later High” for analysis.

Source tiers
Top: the primary source itself (exchange, company, regulator, ETF issuer), Bloomberg, Reuters.
Mid: established crypto media and verified founders or executives.
Low: aggregators, anonymous and influencer accounts.
The full list of SoSoValue source names is kept in the rules and versioned with them.
Take profit and stop loss
TP = 1.5 × and SL = 0.75 × the asset’s typical move over the max hold (from its last 30 days). If TP is under 2× the cost of a trade, the signal is skipped.

When do we get a first answer? The main test pools all news signals: about 100 closed trades, so 3–4 weeks at the current ~30 a week. Results by level or news type are a second look, once each has 30 or more trades. Once there is an average, the page shows how many trades it really needs; a small edge needs more than 100.
A level passes only if it also beats random entries at the same moments, with the same long / short mix, TP, SL and max hold. A rising market makes long news look good; the random comparison takes that out.
