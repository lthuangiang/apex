Khung 4 · How it works › Overview khi đang ở Mainnet

How it works

● Mainnet
OverviewTake profit / Stop lossContext questionsLabelsVersions
Every minute DeskLabs reads new crypto news, keeps articles about a coin listed on SoDEX, asks Claude what kind of news it is, then your mainnet bot decides whether to send a real order to SoDEX from your account. TP and SL are orders on SoDEX. DeskLabs never withdraws or transfers.
1–4 · Collect, Normalize, Score, Contextsame as Paper

Same news, same Claude reader, same context. Paper and mainnet share the signal; only what happens after step 4 is different.

5 · Filtermainnet
your bot's rules + safety checks

Your bot checks its version's rules (markets, min level). Then the safety checks: the bot is running, no other open trade on this coin in your account, size at most 200 USDC.

6 · Decidemainnet
send a real order to SoDEX

DeskLabs sets the coin to 1× isolated on your SoDEX account, then sends a limit IOC order priced to fill now. If nothing fills, the signal is skipped. Builder fee 0.01% applies to each fill.

On SoDEX: TP, SL and max holdmainnet
orders on the exchange

After the entry fills, TP is placed as a reduce-only limit and SL as a stop order on SoDEX, so they stay even if DeskLabs is offline. When one fills, DeskLabs cancels the other. At max hold, DeskLabs closes with a market reduce-only order.

Mainnet tradesmainnet
what you see, what you can do

Each trade shows its status and close reason. You can Pause or Stop the bot, Close now or Edit TP/SL at any time. Mainnet trades are never added to paper results.
