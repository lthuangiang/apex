How we estimate the cost
  • Total cost = SoDEX maker fee + DeskLabs builder fee (0.003%) + price PnL.
  • Price PnL is what quotes lose when the price moves right after they fill. We measure it on the last 24 hours of SoDEX trades: each fill against the mid price 30 seconds later.
  • One price step is the smallest price move, as a share of the price. A bigger step means each round trip can earn more.
  • A 1-min swing that is small compared with one price step usually means cheaper market making.
